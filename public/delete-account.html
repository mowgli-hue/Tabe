Delete your Vlink account

This page explains how to permanently delete your Vlink — Vendor & Restaurant account and the data associated with it. Account deletion is available directly inside the app and on the web dashboard — no email or waiting period required.

Delete from the mobile app

  1. Sign in to the Vlink app
  2. Go to the Home tab and scroll to the bottom
  3. Tap Delete my account
  4. Enter your password to confirm

Delete from the web

  1. Sign in at www.vlink.systems/app
  2. On the Home screen, scroll to the bottom and click Delete my account
  3. Enter your password to confirm

What is deleted

Deletion is immediate and permanent. We delete your account profile (business name, email, password), your catalogue and offers, your orders and order notes, your ledger entries and payment records including uploaded payment screenshots, your pantry data, your business connections and invite codes, and any verification documents you submitted.

What may be kept

Records that belong to the business you transacted with (for example, an order that a connected business placed with you) may remain visible to that other business, without your account attached. We do not retain any other personal data after deletion.

Need help?

If you can't access your account, email admin@vlink.systems from the address you registered with and we'll process the deletion for you.
